THE CONCEPT

EST. 2018

We aren't a typical restaurant. La Fermata is a collision of industrial grit and culinary finesse.

Inspired by the hustle of vintage train stations and the neon glow of modern street food culture, we bring you Lugano's finest Smash Burgers and authentic Tacos. It's loud, it's dark, and the food speaks for itself.

Whether you're grabbing a quick bite before the train or settling in for cocktails, you've arrived at the right stop.
